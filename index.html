How a call gets scored

captured
Cashtag posts from our sweeps are archived with the price, pool and liquidity at that moment — before anyone knows the outcome.
trench only
Majors and deep-liquidity pools are excluded from scoring. A $BTC mention is market commentary, not a trench call, and averaging it in would flatten every number toward nothing.
24h / 7d
Priced again from the same pool. A missed window is recorded as missed — never back-filled with a guess.
pool gone
The traded pool was no longer findable on public trackers at the check. That is what we measured — not a claim about intent.

What the words mean

engagement
Likes, replies and reposts on posts matching a token's symbol, as a 24-hour total.
mentions
How many distinct posts exist. This is the one that separates a crowd from an echo.
🔴 flat
Engagement moved, mentions didn't. It describes those two numbers and nothing else — it is not an accusation of paid promotion.
🟢 organic
Mentions rose with engagement. Never a buy signal.
went dark
No longer listed or findable on the trackers we use at the check. Abandonment looks the same from outside as a pull.
corrections
A correction gets the same reach as the claim it corrects.
